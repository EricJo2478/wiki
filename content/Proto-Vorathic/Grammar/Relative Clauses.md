In [[Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] relative clauses appear directly before the [[Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] they modify. There are no relative [[pronouns]] or markings that signify a relative clause. Instead, a full and complete clause occurs directly before the [[Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] and serves as a its modifier with a gap in place for the target of the relative clause. The embedded [[Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] will have a classifier matching with the target. Examples:
