To turn a sentence into a question in [[Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] you must add the particle xxxx to the end of the sentence.

### Polar Questions

It's that simple. Polar questions simply use the question particle at the end of the sentence. Responses are formed by either repeating the [[Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] as a "yes" or repeating the negative of the [[Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] as a "no".

### WH-Questions

These questions are slightly more complicated. As they should because they ask more complicated things. Though they still require the particle cause why not.

What/Who will take the xxxx (mystery) word to replace the subject/object

How/Why are simple adverbs, nothing fancy.

Where/Whose uses mystery combined with a locative or genitive postposition.

Which functions similar to [[Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Adjectives|adjectives]], coming before the [[Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] group it is restricted to and must agree with classifier.
