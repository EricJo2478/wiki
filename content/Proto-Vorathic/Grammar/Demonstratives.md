Demonstratives in [[Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] are simple. At the end of a [[Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] phrase you can place the [[adverbs]] "here" or "there" to mean proximal or distal respectively.
