# Nouns
`=this.language` nouns are simple they do not inflect at all. Nouns are often, however, followed by a classifier word. Such "power" or "human". These classifiers also often ended up behind a [[Abyssal/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] just for extra clarity.
### Noun Gender
`=this.language` does not have inherint noun gender. Though the following 6 words are often used to classify nouns. 
1. "Power" (to)
2. "Undead" (hiɲ)
3. "Fairy" (kɨg)
4. "Human" (jɨv)
5. "Corpse" (fəg)
6. "Rock" (zɑ)
### Noun Function
`=this.language` has no inflected cases. They are marked using postpositions.
### Noun Number
There is no number distinction marked on nouns. 
### Noun Possession
The genitive postpostion is used to mark possession, coming after the possessor. The possessor comes before the possessee, with the possessee being the head of the genitive phrase. Examples: 
- "human"           "human" ko "arm"      > "the human's arm"
- "demon"           "demon" ko "arm"     > "the demon's arm"
- "fairy"               "fairy" ko "arm"          > "the fairy's arm"
- "I"                     "I" ko "arm"                > "my arm"
