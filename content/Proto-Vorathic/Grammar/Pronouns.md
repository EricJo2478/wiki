# Pronouns
`=this.language` pronouns are simple. There is a 1st person (nɛ) and 2nd person (næ). For the 3rd person the word "thing" is used (boh), often with a [[Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] classifier. These pronouns are interpreted as singular on their own. The plural is created by placing the word "many" after the pronoun as a post nominal modifier.
