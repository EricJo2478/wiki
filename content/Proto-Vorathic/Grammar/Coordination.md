# Coordination
`=this.language` has 3 different coordination strategies. They are all achieved by placing a word after each noun or phrase to mark its meaning. To achieve Coordination (and) use the word "also", for Disjunction (or) use the word "separate" and for Contrast (but) use the word "against".
