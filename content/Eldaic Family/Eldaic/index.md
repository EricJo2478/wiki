This is the language of light. This langauge is rarely spoken, though it is spoken by some light creatures. Eldaic evolved from [[Eldaic Family/Proto-Eldaic/index|Proto-Eldaic]]. Eldaic then split into 2 distinct family branches, resulting in the child languages [[Eldaic Family/Fairish/index|Fairish]] and [[Eldaic Family/Verdant/index|Verdant]]
