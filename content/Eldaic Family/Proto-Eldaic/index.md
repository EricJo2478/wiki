This language is the original language of light. It entered the mortal plane from a plane of light, through the source with first light creatures. Overtime the language of Proto-Eldaic evolved into [[Eldaic Family/Eldaic/index|Eldaic]] and its subsequent child langauges. There are no longer any speakers of this lanuage in my world.
