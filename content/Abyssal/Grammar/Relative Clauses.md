# Relative Clauses
In `=this.language` relative clauses appear directly before the noun they modify. There are no relative pronouns or markings that signify a relative clause. Instead, a full and complete clause occurs directly before the [[Abyssal/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] and serves as a its modifier with a gap in place for the target of the relative clause. The embedded [[Abyssal/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] will agree with the target. Examples:

_**Word forms are ALL created strictly for this example and not real `=this.language` words**_

```gloss
\lbl Subject I
\gla tsutsu tsuu-mu-mu mumu kats-mu
\glb zombie chase-DARK-DARK demon scream.is-DARK
\ft A demon who chased a zombie is screaming
```

```gloss
\lbl Subject II
\gla titi tsutsu tsuu-mu-mu mumu ri-tsu
\glb fairy zombie chase-DARK-DARK demon attack-LIGHT
\ft A fairy is attacking a demon who chased a zombie
```

```gloss
\lbl Direct Object
\gla tsutsu tsuu-mu-mu-lo mumu kats-mu
\glb zombie chase-DARK-DARK-PASSIVE demon scream.is-DARK
\ft A demon whom a zombie chased is screaming
```

```gloss
\lbl Indirect Object
\gla tsutsu ta ka-mu-mu-lo mumu kats-mu
\glb zombie apple give-DARK-DARK-PASSIVE demon scream.is-DARK
\ft A demon whom a zombie gave an apple is screaming
```

```gloss
\lbl Possession
\gla tsutsu re-mu mumu-ko kats-mu
\glb zombie  run-DARK demon-GENITIVE scream.is-DARK
\ft A demon whose zombie ran is screaming
```
