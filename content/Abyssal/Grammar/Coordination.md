# Coordination
`=this.language` has 3 different coordination strategies. They are all achieved by placing a suffix after each noun or phrase to mark its meaning. To achieve Coordination (and) use  _-xxxx_, for Disjunction (or) use _-xxxx_ and for Contrast (but) use  _-xxxx_. 

*It's important to note that because these fused onto words very recently in Abyssal history the fusion happened after consonant voicing harmony and therefore do not interact with the harmony system.*
