# Nouns
`=this.language` nouns come in one of 7 noun [[Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|classes]], which evolved in the Vorathic era before any sound changes took place. There are also a 4 [[Abyssal/Grammar/Cases]] that evolved in the Abyssal era. For the general core grammatical roles, marking will appear on the [[Abyssal/Grammar/Verbs|verb]], rather than the noun. 3 local cases and 1 possessive case will be marked on the noun.
### Noun Gender
`=this.language` has 7 noun [[Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|Classes]]. These noun classes will often occur with class suffixes, although sometimes they will not. Noun class, will therefore, always need to be marked in the lexical entries. See [[Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|Classes]] for more details.
### Noun Function
As stated above `=this.language` has 4 [[Abyssal/Grammar/Cases]]. See [[Abyssal/Grammar/Cases]] for more details. 
### Noun Number
There is no number distinction marked on nouns. 
### Noun Possession
The [[Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|genitive case]] is used to mark possession. The possessor comes before the possessee, with the possessee being the head of the genitive phrase. Examples: 
- "human"           "human"-ko "arm"      > "the human's arm"
- "demon"           "demon"-ko "arm"     > "the demon's arm"
- "fairy"               "fairy"-ko "arm"          > "the fairy's arm"
- "I"                     "I"-ko "arm"                > "my arm"
