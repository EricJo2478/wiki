A [[Vorathic|Vorathic]] language. The language of demon kind.
This is a SOV language. And very head final. Noun [[Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|classes]] that also represent number. Stative verbs. Head marking.

Adjectives and relative clauses precede the nouns they modify. There are postpositions. Possessors will precede possesses. 
# Spoken by
- Demons
- Shadow Charmers
