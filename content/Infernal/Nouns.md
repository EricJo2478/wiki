# Nouns
Nouns in `=this.language` are simpler than in Abyssal. The 7 [[Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|class]] system of Abyssal simplified over time into a 2 [[Infernal/Classes|class]] system. The classes are dark and light. 
### Noun Gender
`=this.language` has 2 noun [[Infernal/Classes]]. These noun classes will often occur with class suffixes, although sometimes they will not. Noun class, will therefore, always need to be marked in the lexical entries. See [[Infernal/Classes]] for more details.
### Noun Function
`=this.language` lost most fo the the [[Infernal/Cases|case]] system found in Abyssal. Therefore things got simpler for nouns, though more complex for locative phrases. There is still a genititve case
### Noun Number
There is no number distinction marked on nouns. 
### Noun Possession
The [[Infernal/Cases|genitive case]] is used to mark possession. The possessor comes before the possessee, with the possessee being the head of the genitive phrase. Examples: 
- "human"           "human"-ko "arm"      > "the human's arm"
- "demon"           "demon"-ko "arm"     > "the demon's arm"
- "fairy"               "fairy"-ko "arm"          > "the fairy's arm"
- "I"                     "I"-ko "arm"                > "my arm"
