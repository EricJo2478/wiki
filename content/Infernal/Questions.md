# Questions
To turn a sentence into a question in `=this.language` you must add the particle xxxx to the end of the sentence. 
### Polar Questions
It's that simple. Polar questions simply use the question particle at the end of the sentence. Responses are formed by either repeating the verb as a "yes" or repeating the negative of the verb as a "no".
### WH-Questions
These questions are slightly more complicated. As they should because they ask more complicated things. Though they still require the particle cause why not.

What/Who will take the xxxx (dark) or xxxx (light) word to replace the subject/object, when noun class is unknown the default is dark. 

How/Why are simple adverbs, nothing fancy.

Where/Whose uses what (dark) combined with the difference noun [[Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|Cases]] from Abyssal. Note that they are not inflection but are separate fossilized words.

Which functions similar to [[Infernal/Adjectives]], coming before the [[Infernal/Nouns|noun]] group it is restricted to and must agree with [[Infernal/Classes|class]].
