Welcome to my conlanging wiki. This is a project where I develop 2 families of conlangs for my Fablehaven inspired Daggerheart campaign. Strong thank you goes to David Peterson and Jessie Sams at Langtime Studio. Their live streams helped me SO much when it came to learning conlanging. Without furthur ado let's take a look at how everything is structured.

There are two language families in this universe. The languge of darkness and the language of light. If you are not familiar with the Fablehaven book series I'll give a brief overview. Basically there is a secret world of magical creatures that can only be seen by people who have "drank the milk" a magical substance that breaks away the veil. In this universe (with some adaptation to work with my TTRPG table) there are 2 magics that flow into the mortal world from 2 immortal planes. The magic of darkness and the magic of light. Hence the 2 language families. The Vorathic Family representing darkness and the Eldaic Family representing light.

Another important historical detail is that within this universe there are 5 monarchs which are great beings of immense power which represent different groups of magical creatures. Here my TTRPG group deviated a bit. In our world there is the Demon King, Fairy Queen, Under King (undead), Dragon King and Earth Mother (nature). Each of these monarchies have a language tree within a family. Additionally each family has a proto form which was spoken in their repective immortal realms. All the languages use the same [[romanization]] and [[glossing]] strategies.  You can see these relationships in the below tables.

##### The Vorathic Family

The vorathic family is comprised of [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] which evolved directly into [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]]. After this stage the family broke off into 3 groups, each with a parent language decended directly from [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] and a child language that decended from the parent.

| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Dragons | [[Vorathic Family/Draconic/index\|Draconic]]         | [[Vorathic Family/Wyrmic/index\|Wyrmic]]         |

| Undead  | [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/index\|Grave Tongue]] | [[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/index\|Shade Cant]] |
