### Sound Changes (Proto: [[Proto-Eldaic/Phonology|Proto-Eldaic]])

| Name                       | Change                                   | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stop Devoicing             | C[-cont] > [-voice] / _,C[-cont, -voice] | Voiced stops devoice next to voiceless stops.                                                                                                                                                |
| Glottal Devoicing          |                                          | Glottals universally gets devoiced                                                                                                                                                           |
| Pre-Nasal Neutralization   |                                          | Certain vowels change their quality before nasal codas. Low vowels raise (so *æ > ɛ and *ɑ > ɔ), and high vowels lower (*i > e, *u > o, *ɨ > ə, *y > ø).                                     |
| Loss of Z                  |                                          | z to d after vowels and word initially, to s everywhere else                                                                                                                                 |
| Mid Vowel Spread           | V[αATR, -nasal] > [αhigher]              | The [-ATR] vowels lower, resulting in *ɛ becoming æ, *ɔ becoming ɑ. On the [+ATR] side, *e raises to i, *o raises to u, and *ø raises to y. Note: This rule leaves the central vowels alone. |
| Central Vowel Annihilation | V[-back] > [+front] / #(C)_              | Central vowels become front vowels, resulting in *ə becoming e and *ɨ becoming i.                                                                                                            |
