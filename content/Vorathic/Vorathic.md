This is the language of dark. The [[Draconic|Draconic]], [[Grave Tongue|Grave Tongue]] and [[Abyssal/Abyssal|Infernal]] languages directly descend from Vorathic. Also known by some as the secret undead language.

Vorathic is a head final language with a noun [[Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|class]] system that merged right before sound changes started. There is no case system.

# Spoken by
- Liches
- Underlings
