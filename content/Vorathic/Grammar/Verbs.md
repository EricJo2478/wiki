# Verbs
Verbs in `=this.language` to a lot of heavy lifting. They agree with noun [[Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|class]], both the subject and direct object (if present). The conjugate for voice (passive/active), polarity (positive/negative), and tense (past/present/future).
### Transitivity
Verbs in `=this.language` have 3 different sets of infection: Intransitive, Active Transitive, Passive Transitive. Each of these transitivity types takes a different set of verbal suffixes (containing tense and polarity), which come from old auxiliaries. Between these inflections and the verb root agreement suffixes also form, see those below. Examples of intransitive verb constructions:

- SUBJECT"demon" VERB"attack"-SUB-INF"pos-present"    "the demon is attacking"
### Agreement
In the above example SUB is agreement for class I, since intransitive verbs agree with their subjects. Transitive verbs agree with their subject followed by their object. 
### Polarity/Mood
Polarity is simply a different inflection in `=this.language`. See the reference tables. Mood is not marked on the verb root or suffixes in `=this.language`, but is instead conveyed through following auxiliary verbs.
### Tense
Tense is handled similarly to polarity. As seen in the tables below the suffix changes based on tense.
### Copula
`=this.language` uses a zero copula. For the present tense the verb is omitted entirely in the Positive. For other tenses use the Intransitive suffix alone with no verb root as the copular verb.
