# Nouns
`=this.language` nouns come in one of 7 noun [[Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|classes]], which evolved in the Vorathic era before any sound changes took place. For the general core grammatical roles, marking will appear on the [[Vorathic/Grammar/Verbs|verb]], rather than the noun. Cases are all marked with postpositions.
### Noun Gender
`=this.language` has 7 noun [[Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|Classes]]. These noun classes will often occur with class suffixes, although sometimes they will not. Noun class, will therefore, always need to be marked in the lexical entries. See [[Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|Classes]] for more details.
### Noun Function
As stated above `=this.language` has no inflected cases. They are marked using postpositions.
### Noun Number
There is no number distinction marked on nouns. 
### Noun Possession
The genitive postpostion is used to mark possession, coming after the possessor. The possessor comes before the possessee, with the possessee being the head of the genitive phrase. Examples: 
- "human"           "human" ko "arm"      > "the human's arm"
- "demon"           "demon" ko "arm"     > "the demon's arm"
- "fairy"               "fairy" ko "arm"          > "the fairy's arm"
- "I"                     "I" ko "arm"                > "my arm"
