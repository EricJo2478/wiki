# Coordination
`=this.language` has 3 different coordination strategies. They are all achieved by placing a particle after each noun or phrase to mark its meaning. To achieve Coordination (and) use the particle _xxxx_, for Disjunction (or) use the particle _xxxx_ and for Contrast (but) use the particle _xxxx_.
