This is the language of darkness. This langauge is rarely spoken, though it is spoken by some dark creatures. Vorathic evolved from [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]]. Vorathic then split into 3 distinct family branches, resulting in the child languages [[Vorathic Family/Draconic/index|Draconic]], [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/index|Grave Tongue]], [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]]

Vorathic is a SOV head final language with a [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|class]] system that merged right before sound changes started. You can see its [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Phonology|phonology]], [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Derivational Morphology|derivational morphology]] and [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/index|grammar]] at these respective links. For all the words in the language see [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Parts of Speech|parts of speech]]
