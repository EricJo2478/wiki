The [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] phonology has a very small vowel inventory. The syllable structure is (C)(C)(C)V(C)(C).  You can find a sound frequency chart below the consonant and vowel tables. For the sound changes that created this phonology from [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/Phonology/index|Proto-Vorathic]] see [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Phonology/Sound Changes|Sound Changes]].
