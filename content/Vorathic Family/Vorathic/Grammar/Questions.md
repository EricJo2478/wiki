To turn a sentence into a question in [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] you must add the particle xxxx to the end of the sentence.

### Polar Questions

It's that simple. Polar questions simply use the question particle at the end of the sentence. Responses are formed by either repeating the [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] as a "yes" or repeating the negative of the [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] as a "no".

### WH-Questions

These questions are slightly more complicated. As they should because they ask more complicated things. Though they still require the particle cause why not.

What/Who will take the xxxx (mystery) word to replace the subject/object, when [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|noun class]] is unknown the default is class VII.

How/Why are simple [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Adverbs|adverbs]], nothing fancy.

Where/Whose uses mystery (class VII) combined with the a locative or genitive postposition.

Which functions similar to [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Adjectives|adjectives]], coming before the [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] group it is restricted to and must agree with [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|class]].
