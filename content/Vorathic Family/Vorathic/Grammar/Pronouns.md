[[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] pronouns are simple. There is a 1st person and 2nd person which each have a singluar and plural form. The 3rd person pronouns are shown with their class agreement in the [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|noun classes]] table.

| Singular | na         | na         |
