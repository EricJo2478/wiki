[[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] nouns come in one of 7 [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|noun classes]], which evolved in the Vorathic era before any sound changes took place. For the general core grammatical roles, marking will appear on the [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Verbs|verb]], rather than the noun. Cases are all marked with postpositions.

### Noun Gender

[[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] has 7 [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|noun classes]]. These noun classes will often occur with class suffixes, although sometimes they will not. Noun class, will therefore, always need to be marked in the lexical entries. See [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|Noun Classes]] for more details.

### Noun Function

As stated above [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] has no inflected cases. They are marked using postpositions.

### Noun Number

There is no number distinction marked on nouns.

### Noun Possession

The genitive postpostion is used to mark possession, coming after the possessor. The possessor comes before the possessee, with the possessee being the head of the genitive phrase. Examples:

- "human"           "human" ko "arm"      > "the human's arm"
- "demon"           "demon" ko "arm"     > "the demon's arm"
- "fairy"               "fairy" ko "arm"          > "the fairy's arm"
- "I"                     "I" ko "arm"                > "my arm"
