[[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] nouns come in one of 6 [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|noun classes]], which evolved in the Vorathic era before any sound changes took place. For the general core grammatical roles, marking will appear on the [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Verbs|verb]], rather than the noun. Cases are all marked with postpositions.

### Noun Gender

[[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] has 6 [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|noun classes]]. These noun classes will often occur with class suffixes, although sometimes they will not. Noun class, will therefore, always need to be marked in the lexical entries.

### Noun Function

As stated above [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] has no inflected cases. They are marked using postpositions.

### Noun Number

There is no number distinction marked on nouns.

### Noun Possession

The [[sa|genitive postposition]] is used to mark possession, coming after the possessor. The possessor comes before the possessee, with the possessee being the head of the genitive phrase. An example below:

<table class="gloss">
  <tr>
    <td>t'o</td>
    <td>sa</td>
    <td>va</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>human</td>
    <td><span class="sc">GEN</span></td>
    <td>apple</td>
  </tr>
</table>
<p class="gloss-ft">“a human's apple”</p>
