[[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] has a group of stative [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Verbs|verbs]] that perform the function of adjectival predicates. They are intransitive, by default, and take the present morphology. This is the basic method of describing any object. The past/future morphology can be used to indicate past/future state for the adjective being used.

### Placement

An adjective can be used as a prenominal modifier. To do so they appear in a [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Relative Clauses|relative clause]]. See an example bellow:

<table class="gloss">
  <tr>
    <td>two</td>
    <td>tag-a</td>
    <td>toga</td>
    <td>to-lor-ga</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>fairy</td>
    <td>to be strong<span class="sc">.PRS-I</span></td>
    <td>demon</td>
    <td>attack<span class="sc">.PRS-III-I</span></td>
  </tr>
</table>
<p class="gloss-ft">“The fairy attacks the strong demon”</p>
