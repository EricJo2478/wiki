Verbs in [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] to a lot of heavy lifting. They agree with [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|noun class]], both the subject and direct object (if present). They also conjugate for voice (passive/active), polarity (positive/negative), and tense (past/present/future).

### Transitivity

Verbs in [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] have 3 different sets of infection: Intransitive, Active Transitive, Passive Transitive. Each of these transitivity types takes a different set of verbal suffixes (containing tense and polarity), which come from old auxiliaries. Between these inflections and the verb root agreement suffixes also form, see those below. Examples of intransitive verb constructions:

- SUBJECT"demon" VERB"attack"-SUB-INF"pos-present"    "the demon is attacking"

### Agreement

In the above example SUB is agreement for [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|class]] I, since intransitive verbs agree with their subjects. Transitive verbs agree with their subject followed by their object.

### Polarity/Mood

Polarity is simply a different inflection in [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]]. See the reference tables. Mood is not marked on the verb root or suffixes in [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]], but is instead conveyed through following auxiliary verbs.

### Tense

Tense is handled similarly to polarity. As seen in the tables below the suffix changes based on tense.

### Copula

[[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] uses a zero copula. For the present tense the verb is omitted entirely in the Positive. For other tenses use the Intransitive suffix alone with no verb root as the copular verb.
