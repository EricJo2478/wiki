[[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] has 3 different coordination strategies. They are all achieved by placing a particle after each [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] or phrase to mark its meaning. To achieve Coordination (and) use the particle [[zo'a]], for Disjunction (or) use the particle [[pu]] and for Contrast (but) use the particle [[zto]].
