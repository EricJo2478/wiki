# Demonstratives

Demonstratives in [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] are simple. At the end of a [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] phrase you can place the particles [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Lexicon/ngo]] or [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Lexicon/ko]] to mean proximal or distal respectively.

_Note that these are also used as locational [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Adverbs|adverbs]]_
