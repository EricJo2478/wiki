This is the demon lanuage. It is commonly spoken among true demons and powerful creatures among demon culture. Abyssal evolved from [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]].

Abyssal is a very head final SOV language. It features a consonant voicing harmony in which consonants take on the voicing of the final consonant in the word.  [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Adjectives|Adjectives]] and [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Relative Clauses|relative clauses]] precede the [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Nouns|nouns]] they modify. There are postpositions. Possessors will precede possesses. You can see its [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Phonology|phonology]], [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Derivational Morphology|derivational morphology]] [[orthography]] and [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/index|grammar]] at these respective links.
