There are 3 cases each pulling double duty (plus the genitive). Where context cannot differentiate additional phrases will be required. Below are the cases and their proto sources. The suffixes only fused after the [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] language (right before the first [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Phonology|Abyssal sound changes]].).

| Case                                   | Postpos | Proto (Vorathic) | Meaning |
| -------------------------------------- | ------- | ---------------- | ------- |
| Genitive                               | -së     | -[[sa]]          | hand    |
| Allative (Adessive) "to" / "on"        | -tsu̇   | -[[tsu]]         | mouth   |
| Ablative (Abessive) "from" / "without" | -të     | -[[ta]]          | back    |
| Illative (Inessive) "in" / "into"      | -fë     | -[[fa]]          | belly   |
_Note: Irregular case declentions will be noted in the lexical entries. Voicing harmony is not marked and behaves as expected. Additionally all ö final nouns will change to be u̇ final in the Illative case. This is considered a regular change and will not be noted in lexcial entries._
