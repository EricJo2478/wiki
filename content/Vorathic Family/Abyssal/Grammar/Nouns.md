[[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]] nouns come in one of 6 [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|noun classes]], which evolved in the [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] era before any sound changes took place. There are also a 4 [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|cases]] that evolved in the [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]] era. For the general core grammatical roles, marking will appear on the [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Verbs|verb]], rather than the [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Nouns|noun]]. 3 local [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|cases]] and 1 possessive [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|case]] will be marked on the [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Nouns|noun]].

### Noun Gender

[[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]] has 6 [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|noun classes]]. These [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|noun classes]] will often occur with class suffixes, although sometimes they will not. [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|Noun class]], will therefore, always need to be marked in the lexical entries.

### Noun Function

As stated above [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]] has 4 [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|cases]]. See [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|Cases]] for more details.

### Noun Number

There is no number distinction marked on nouns.

### Noun Possession

The [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|genitive case]] is used to mark possession. The possessor comes before the possessee, with the possessee being the head of the genitive phrase. Examples:

<table class="gloss">
  <tr>
    <td>t'ö-së</td>
    <td>vë</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>human<span class="sc">-GEN</span></td>
    <td>apple</td>
  </tr>
</table>
<p class="gloss-ft">“a human's apple”</p>
