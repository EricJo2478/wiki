Demonstratives in [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]] are simple. At the end of a [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] phrase you can place the particles [[ngö]] or [[kö]] to mean proximal or distal respectively.
