[[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]] has 3 different coordination strategies. They are all achieved by placing a suffix after each [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] or phrase to mark its meaning. To achieve Coordination (and) use  _-xxxx_, for Disjunction (or) use _-xxxx_ and for Contrast (but) use  _-xxxx_.

_It's important to note that because these fused onto words very recently in Abyssal history the fusion happened after consonant voicing harmony and therefore do not interact with the harmony system._
