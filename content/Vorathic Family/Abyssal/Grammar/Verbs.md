Verbs in [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]] do a lot of heavy lifting. They agree with [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|noun class]], for the subject. They [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Conjugation|conjugate]] for voice (passive/active), polarity (positive/negative), mood (generic/imperative/refusal/indifference), and tense (past/present/future).

### Transitivity

Verbs in [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]] have 3 different sets of infection: Intransitive, Active Transitive, Passive Transitive. Each of these transitivity types takes a different set of verbal suffixes (containing tense, polarity and mood), which come from old auxiliaries. Between these inflections and the verb root agreement suffixes also form, see those below.

### Agreement
In the above example _du̇d_ is agreement for [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|class]] III, since verbs agree with their subjects.
### Polarity/Mood
Polarity and mood are handled the same in [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]]. In the positive there is the Generic or the Imperative. In the negative we distinguish between Generic, Refusal or Indifference. All of the forms for the different transitivities are in the [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Conjugation|conjugation]] tables.
### Tense
Tense is handled similarly to polarity and mood. As seen in the [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Conjugation|conjugation]] tables suffix changes based on tense.
### Copula
[[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]] uses a zero copula. For the present tense the verb is omitted entirely in the Positive Generic. For other tenses use the Intransitive suffix alone with no verb root as the copular verb.
