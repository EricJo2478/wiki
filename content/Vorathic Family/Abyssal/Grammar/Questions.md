To turn a sentence into a question in [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]] you must add the particle [[ngu̇]] to the end of the sentence.

### Polar Questions

It's that simple. Polar questions simply use the question particle at the end of the sentence. Responses are formed by either repeating the [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] as a "yes" or repeating the negative of the [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] as a "no".

### WH-Questions

These questions are slightly more complicated. As they should because they ask more complicated things. Though they still require the particle cause why not.

What/Who will take the [[bu̇vu̇]] word to replace the subject/object. When [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|noun class]] is unknown the default is VI.

[[ngu̇ku̇|How]]/[[ngu̇bëg|Why]] are simple [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Adverbs|adverbs]], nothing fancy.

Where/Whose use [[bu̇vu̇]] combined with the different [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|noun cases]].

[[ngu̇vu̇|Which]] functions similarly to [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Adjectives|adjectives]], coming before the [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] group it is restricted to and must agree with [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|class]].
