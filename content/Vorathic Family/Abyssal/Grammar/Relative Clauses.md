In [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]] relative clauses appear directly before the [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] they modify. There are no relative [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Pronouns|pronouns]] or markings that signify a relative clause. Instead, a full and complete clause occurs directly before the [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] and serves as its modifier with a gap in place for the target of the relative clause. The embedded [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] will agree with the target. Note that for possesion a free standing _së_ comes at at the begining of the relative clause. Examples:
