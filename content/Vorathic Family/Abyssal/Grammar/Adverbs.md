[[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]] has 3 types of adverbs.

### Manner

Manner adverbs evolved using this proto construction. Stative [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] suffixed with the power [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|class]] and the [[zë|comitative postposition]]. This over time became less transparent and they now are their own lexical entries.

### Locational

Many place [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Nouns|nouns]] can be turned into locational adverbs by means of locative [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|cases]]. There are 2 basic locational adverbs [[pu̇tsu̇ngö]] "here" and [[pu̇tsu̇kö]] "there". These can appear in the allative, ablative, and illative [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|cases]].

|          | Allative       | Ablative     | Illative     |
| -------- | -------------- | ------------ | ------------ |
| Proximal | [[pu̇tsu̇ngö]] | [[pu̇tëngö]] | [[pu̇fëngö]] |
| Distal   | [[pu̇tsu̇kö]]  | [[pu̇tëkö]]  | [[pu̇fëkö]]  |
For the generic locative sense of "here" or "there" the allative form is used. These adverbs differ in that they are primarily used for motion or for emphasis.

### Temporal

In proto temporal adverbs are constructed using the locational roots. When a root is followed by a locative postposition it forms a temporal adverb construction. For example "here" followed by the illative postpostion would mean "now". In [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]] the temporal adverbs and their postpostions have fused into their own lexican entries.
