Verbs in [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/index|Grave Tongue]] do a lot of heavy lifting. They also conjugate for voice (passive/active), polarity (positive/negative), and tense (past/present/future).

### Transitivity

Verbs in [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/index|Grave Tongue]] have 3 different sets of inflection: Intransitive, Active Transitive, Passive Transitive. Each of these transitivity types takes a different set of verbal suffixes (containing tense and polarity), which come from old auxiliaries.

### Polarity

Polarity is simply a different conjugation in [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/index|Grave Tongue]].

### Tense

Tense is handled similiarly to polarity.

### Copula

[[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/index|Grave Tongue]] uses a zero copula. For the present tense the verb is omitted entirely in the positive. For other tenses or polarities use the intransitive suffix alone with no verb root as the copular verb.
