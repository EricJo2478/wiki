There are 3 types of adverbs in [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/index|Grave Tongue]].

### Manner

Manner adverbs evolved using this proto construction. A stative [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] suffixed with the power [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/Grammar/Classes|class]] and the comitative postpostition. This overtime became less transparent and they now have indivifual lexical entries.

### Locational

Many place [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/Grammar/Nouns|nouns]] can be turned into locational adverbs by means of locative postpostions. There are 2 basic locational adverbs _xxxx_ "here" and _xxxx_ "there". these often appear with the allative, ablative and illative post positions. The following forms have fused over time due to frequent use.

|                 | Standard | Allative | Ablative | Illative |
| --------------- | -------- | -------- | -------- | -------- |
| Proximal (here) | _xxxx_   | _xxxx_   | _xxxx_   | _xxxx_   |
| Distal (there)  | _xxxx_   | _xxxx_   | _xxxx_   | _xxxx_   |
For the generic locative sense of _xxxx_ or _xxxx_ the stanard form is used. Something like the allative would also be used for the locative for a standard place noun. These adverbs differ in that they are primarily used for motion or for emphasis.

### Temporal

In proto temporal adverbs are constructed using the locational roots. When a root is followed by a locative postposition it forms a temporal adverb construction. For example "here" followed by the illative postposition would mean "now". In [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/index|Grave Tongue]] the temporal adverbs and their postpositions have fused into their own lexical entries.
