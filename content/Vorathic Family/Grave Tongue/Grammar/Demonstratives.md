Demonstratives in [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/index|Grave Tongue]] are simple. At the end of a [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] phrase you can plase you can place the words _xxxx_ or _xxxx_ to mean proximal or distal respectively.

_Note that these are also used as locational adverbs._
