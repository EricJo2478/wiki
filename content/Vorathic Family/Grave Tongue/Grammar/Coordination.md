[[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/index|Grave Tongue]] has 3 different coordination strategies. They are all achieved by placing a word between each [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] or phrase to convey its meaning. To achieve Coordination (and) use _xxxx_, for Disjunction (or) use _xxxx_, and for Contrast (but) use _xxxx_.

_Note: This is slightly different from Vorathic where **each** noun the coordinator after it. In Grave Tongue it only comes **between**  nouns._
