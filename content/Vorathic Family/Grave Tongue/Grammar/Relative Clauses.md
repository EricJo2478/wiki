In [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/index|Grave Tongue]] relative clause appear directly before the [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] they modify. There are no relative [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/Grammar/Pronouns|pronouns]] or markings that signify a relative clause. Instead, a full and complete clause occurs directly before the [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] and serves as its modifier with a gap in place for the target of the relative clause. Note that for possession the _xxxx_ particle comes at the beginning of the relative clause. Examples:
