Demonstratives in [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/index|Infernal]] are simple. At the end of a [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] phrase you can place the particles [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Lexicon/nga|nga]] or [[ka]] to mean proximal or distal respectively.
