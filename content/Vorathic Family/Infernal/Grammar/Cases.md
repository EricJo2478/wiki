There is only 1 case in [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/index|Infernal]], the genitive. Where context cannot differenetiate additional phrases will be required. This means where these missing cases were needed postpostions evolved to still convey that meaning. Below is the case and its proto source. The suffix is only fused after the [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] language (right before the first [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Phonology|Abyssal sound change]].).

| Case                | Suffix  | Proto (Vorathic) | Meaning |
| ------------------- | ------- | ---------------- | ------- |
| Genitive            | -ko     | -ko              | hand    |
