The pronouns in [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/index|Infernal]] also lost their [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Cases|case]] distinctions and are now just in the core and genitive forms. The 1st person pronoun is [[da]], the 2nd person pronoun is [[be'a]], the dark 3rd person pronoun is [[bad]] and the light 3rd person pronoun is [[bed]].
