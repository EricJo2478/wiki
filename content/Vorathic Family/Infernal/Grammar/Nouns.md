Nouns in [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/index|Infernal]] are simpler than in [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]]. The 7 [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Classes|class system of Abyssal]] simplified over time into a 2 [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Classes|class]] system. The classes are dark and light.

### Noun Gender

[[Vorathic Family/Infernal/index|Infernal]] has 2 [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Classes|noun classes]]. These [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Classes|noun classs]] will often occur with class suffixes, although sometimes they will not. Noun class, will therefore, always need to be marked in the lexical entries.

### Noun Function

[[Vorathic Family/Infernal/index|Infernal]] lost most of the the [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Cases|case]] system found in [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]]. Therefore things got simpler for [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Nouns|nouns]], though more complex for locative phrases. There is still a [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Cases|genititve case]].

### Noun Number

There is no number distinction marked on nouns.

### Noun Possession

The [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Cases|genitive case]] is used to mark possession. The possessor comes before the possessee, with the possessee being the head of the genitive phrase. Examples:

- "human"           "human"-ko "arm"      > "the human's arm"
- "demon"           "demon"-ko "arm"     > "the demon's arm"
- "fairy"               "fairy"-ko "arm"          > "the fairy's arm"
- "I"                     "I"-ko "arm"                > "my arm"
