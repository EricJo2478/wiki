[[Vorathic Family/Infernal/index|Infernal]] has 3 types of adverbs.

### Manner

Manner adverbs evolved using this proto construction. Stative [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Verbs|Verb]] suffixed with the power [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Classes|class]] and the comitative postposition. This overtime became less transparent.

### Locational

Many place [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Nouns|nouns]] can be turned into locational adverbs by means of locative [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|cases from Abyssal]]. This has become less obvious with the loss of the locative [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Cases|cases in Infernal]]. There are 2 basic locational adverbs xxxx "here" and xxxx "there". These can appear in the allative, ablative, and illative [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|cases from Abyssal]], however these are not inflections, but fossilized ancient words.

|          | Allative              | Ablative              | Illative              |
| -------- | --------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| Proximal | 3rd p + ALL + "here"  | 3rd p + ABL + "here"  | 3rd p + ILL + "here"  |
| Distal   | 3rd p + ALL + "there" | 3rd p + ABL + "there" | 3rd p + ILL + "there" |
For the generic locative sense of "here" or "there" the allative form is used. These adverbs differ in that they are primarily used for motion or for emphasis.

### Temporal

In proto temporal adverbs are constructed using the locational roots. When a root is followed by a locative postpostition it forms a temporal adverb construction. For example "here" followed by the illative postpostion would mean "now". In [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/index|Infernal]] the temporal adverbs and their postpostions have fused into their own lexican entries.
