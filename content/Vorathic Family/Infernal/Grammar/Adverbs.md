[[Vorathic Family/Infernal/index|Infernal]] has 3 types of adverbs.

### Manner

Manner adverbs evolved using this proto construction. Stative [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Verbs|Verb]] suffixed with the power [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Classes|class]] and the comitative postposition. This over time became less transparent.

### Locational

Many place [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Nouns|nouns]] can be turned into locational adverbs by means of locative [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|cases from Abyssal]]. This has become less obvious with the loss of the locative [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Cases|cases in Infernal]]. Therefore these locational adverbs have their own lexical entries. There are 2 basic locational adverbs [[petsenga]] "here" and [[petseka]] "there". These can appear in the allative, ablative, and illative [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Grammar/Cases|cases from Abyssal]], however these are not inflections, but fossilized ancient words.

|          | Allative     | Ablative    | Illative    |
| -------- | ------------ | ----------- | ----------- |
| Proximal | [[petsenga]] | [[petenga]] | [[pofenga]] |
| Distal   | [[petseka]]  | [[peteka]]  | [[pofeka]]  |
For the generic locative sense of "here" or "there" the allative form is used. These adverbs differ in that they are primarily used for motion or for emphasis.

### Temporal

In proto temporal adverbs are constructed using the locational roots. When a root is followed by a locative postposition it forms a temporal adverb construction. For example "here" followed by the illative postpostion would mean "now". In [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/index|Infernal]] the temporal adverbs and their postpostions have fused into their own lexical entries.
