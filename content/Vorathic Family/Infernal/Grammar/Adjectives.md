[[Vorathic Family/Infernal/index|Infernal]] has a group of stative verbs that perform the function of adjectival predicates. They are intransitive, by default, will take the present morphology. This is the basic method of describing any object. The past morphology can be used to indicate the past state for the adjective being used.

### Placement

An adjective can be used as a prenominal modifier. To do so they appear in a [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Relative Clauses|relative clause]]. See an example below:

<table class="gloss">
  <tr>
    <td>pta</td>
    <td>deg-e</td>
    <td>dage</td>
    <td>da-ded</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>fairy</td>
    <td>to be strong<span class="sc">-DRK</span></td>
    <td>demon</td>
    <td>attack<span class="sc">.PRS-LGT</span></td>
  </tr>
</table>
<p class="gloss-ft">“The fairy attacks the strong demon”</p>
### Comparison (X is more Y than Z)
Comparison in [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/index|Infernal]] is done using the verb [[mo|to be more than]] to compare the subject to the object. Typically the subject is a genitive to convey what aspect is being compared. It is important to note that because this evolved from a construction involving "to kneel" this specific construction uses a OSV word order.
<table class="gloss">
  <tr>
    <td>pta</td>
    <td>dage-se</td>
    <td>dege</td>
    <td>ma-ge</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>fairy</td>
    <td>demon<span class="sc">-GEN</span></td>
    <td>strength</td>
    <td>to be more than<span class="sc">-DRK</span></td>
  </tr>
</table>
<p class="gloss-ft">“The demon is stronger than the fairy”</p>
