[[Vorathic Family/Infernal/index|Infernal]] has a group of stative verbs that perform the function of adjectival predicates. They are intransitive, by default, will take the present morphology. This is the basic method of describing any object. The past morphology can be used to indicate past state for the adjective being used.

### Placement

An adjective can be used as a prenominal modifier. To do so they appear in a [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Relative Clauses|relative clause]]. See an example bellow:
