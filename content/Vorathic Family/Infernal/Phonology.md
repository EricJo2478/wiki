The [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Infernal]] phonology has a very small vowel inventory. The syllable structure is (C)(C)(C)V(C)(C).  You can find a sound frequency chart below the consonant and vowel tables followed by the sound change charge. The proto for this phonology is the [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/Phonology|Abyssal Phonology]].
