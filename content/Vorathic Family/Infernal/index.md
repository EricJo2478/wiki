This is the lesser demon lanuage. It is commonly spoken among dark creatures with proximity to demons. Infernal evolved from [[Vorathic Family/Abyssal/index|Abyssal]].

Infernal is a very head final SOV language. It features a consonant voicing harmony in which consonants take on the voicing of the final consonant in the word.  [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Adjectives|Adjectives]] and [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Relative Clauses|relative clauses]] precede the [[Vorathic Family/Infernal/Grammar/Nouns|nouns]] they modify. There are postpositions. Possessors will precede possesses.
