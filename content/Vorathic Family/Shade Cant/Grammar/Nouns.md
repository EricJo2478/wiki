[[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/index|Shade Cant]] nouns are quire simple. For their core grammatical roles marking will appear on the [[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/Grammar/Verbs|verb]]. All [[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/Grammar/Cases|cases]] except the gentivie are marked with postpositions.

### Number

There is no number distinction marked on nouns.

### Possession

The genitive [[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/Grammar/Cases|case]] is used to mark possession, coming after the possessor. The possessor comes before the possessee, with the possessee being the head of the genitive phrase. An example below:

<table class="gloss">
  <tr>
    <td>test</td>
    <td>testa</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>test</td>
    <td>test-<span class="sc">A</span></td>
  </tr>
</table>
<p class="gloss-ft">“testing a thing”</p>
