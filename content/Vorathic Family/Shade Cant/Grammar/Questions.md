To turn a sentance into a question in [[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/index|Shade Cant]] you must add the particle _xxxx_ to the end of the sentence.

### Polar Questions

It's that simple. Polar questions simply use the question particle at the end of the sentence. Responses are formed by either repeating the [[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] as a "yes" or repeating the negative of the [[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] as a "no".

### WH-Questions

These questions are slightly more complicated. As described below.

What/Who will take the _xxxx_ word to replace the subject/object.

How/Why/Where/Whose use simple adverbs.

Which functions similar to [[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/Grammar/Adjectives|adjectives]], coming before the [[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] group it is restricted to.
