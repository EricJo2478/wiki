Demonstratives in [[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/index|Shade Cant]] came from [[Vorathic Family/Grave Tongue/index|Grave Tongue]] 3rd person pronouns. Therefore held on the class system of [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] and have 5 forms depending on what is being referenced. The animate 3rd person pronoun remained a pronoun as the only one.
