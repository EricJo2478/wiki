Verbs in [[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/index|Shade Cant]] do a lot of heavy lifting. They conjugate for voice (passive/active), polarity (positive/negative), and tense (past/present/future).

### Transitivity

Verbs in [[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/index|Shade Cant]] have 3 different sets of inflection: Intransitive, Active Transitive, Passive Transitive. Each of these transitivity types takes a different set of verbal suffixes (containing tense and polarity), which come from old auxiliaries.

### Polarity

Polarity is simply a different conjugation in [[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/index|Shade Cant]].

### Tense

Tense is handled similiarly to polarity.

### Copula

[[Vorathic Family/Shade Cant/index|Shade Cant]] uses a zero copula. For the present tense the verb is omitted entirely in the positive. For other tenses or polarities use the intransitive suffix alone with no verb root as the copular verb.
