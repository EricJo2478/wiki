Derivational Morphology in [[index|Proto-Vorathic]] relies primarily on root compounding. This is where 2 roots combine to form a new word. Below are the primary categories.

| Name         | Form          | Meaning                          | POS  |
| ------------ | ------------- | -------------------------------- | ---- |
| Agentive     | VERB+demon    | being who does VERB              | NOUN |
| Locative     | VERB+place    | place where VERB happens         | NOUN |
| Instrumental | VERB+tool     | thing used to do VERB            | NOUN |
| Abstract     | ADJ+hhë       | thing of ADJ quality             | NOUN |
| Augmentative | NOUN+mountain | bigger version of NOUN           | NOUN |
| Diminutive   | NOUN+child    | smaller version of NOUN          | NOUN |
| Causative    | ADJ+to push   | to cause ADJ quality on NOUN     | VERB |
| Reversative  | VERB+ablative | to do the reverse action of VERB | VERB |
