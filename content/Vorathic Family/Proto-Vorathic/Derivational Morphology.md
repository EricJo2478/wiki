Derivational Morphology in [[index|Proto-Vorathic]] relies primarily on root compounding. This is where 2 roots combine to form a new word. Below are the primary categories, including noun classifiers.

| Name             | Form         | Meaning                                                                                         | POS  |
| ---------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---- |
| Agentive         | VERB+tegë    | being who does VERB                                                                             | NOUN |
| Locative         | VERB+ngà     | place where VERB happens                                                                        | NOUN |
| Instrumental     | VERB+ràb     | thing used to do VERB                                                                           | NOUN |
| Abstract         | ADJ+hhë      | thing of ADJ quality                                                                            | NOUN |
| Augmentative     | NOUN+wëd     | bigger version of NOUN                                                                          | NOUN |
| Diminutive       | NOUN+vu      | smaller version of NOUN                                                                         | NOUN |
| Causative        | ADJ+su       | to cause ADJ quality on NOUN                                                                    | VERB |
| Reversative      | VERB+të      | to do the reverse action of VERB                                                                | VERB |
| Collective       | NOUN+hoetegë | a horde/group of NOUN                                                                           | NOUN |
| Void             | NOUN+soengà  | what's left in absence of NOUN, or the opposite of NOUN                                         | NOUN |
| Demon            | NOUN+gë      | the demon version of NOUN, or something that is part of a being (since demons are the speakers) | NOUN |
| Darkness         | NOUN+nod     | the dark version of NOUN. Such as evil, greedy or self focused version                          | NOUN |
| Light            | NOUN+loer    | the light version of NOUN. Such as help, comunity, support.                                     | NOUN |
| Instrument       | NOUN+hè      | the tool version of NOUN.                                                                       | NOUN |
| Broken           | NOUN+wà      | the broken version of NOUN.                                                                     | NOUN |
| Raw/Abstract     | NOUN+hü      | the raw or basic version of NOUN.                                                               | NOUN |
| Manner           | ADJ+tàhhëzae | the adverb meaning to perform an action in ADJ manner                                           | ADV  |
| To location      | ti+NOUN      | the adverb meaning to perform an action going to NOUN                                           | ADV  |
| From location    | të+NOUN      | the adverb meaning to perform an action going from NOUN                                         | ADV  |
| In location      | fà+NOUN      | the adverb meaning to perform an action in NOUN                                                 | ADV  |
| Temporal present | NOUN+ti      | a temporal adverb in the present relative to the NOUN. (example: day = today)                   | ADV  |
| Temporal future  | NOUN+të      | a temporal adverb in the future relative to the NOUN. (example: day = tomorrow)                 | ADV  |
| Temporal past    | NOUN+fà      | a temporal adverb in the past relative to the NOUN. (example: day = yesterday)                  | ADV  |
