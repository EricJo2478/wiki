- **Pronunciation (IPA):** ɦy
- **Part of Speech:** verb
- **Root:** hhü

### Definitions

- (sv.) having very little or no light.
- (sv.) radiating, admitting, or reflecting little light.
- (sv.) approaching black in hue.
