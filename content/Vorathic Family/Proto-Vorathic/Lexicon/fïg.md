- **Pronunciation (IPA):** fɨg
- **Part of Speech:** verb
- **Root:** fïg

### Definitions

- (v.) to present voluntarily and without expecting compensation; bestow.
- (v.) to hand to someone.
- (v.) to place in someone's care.

* (v.) to impart or communicate.
* (v.) to set forth or show; present; offer.
* (v.) to furnish, provide, or proffer.
* (v.) to bestow (the object of one's choice) upon, as if by providence.
