- **Pronunciation (IPA):** ɦe
- **Part of Speech:** verb
- **Root:** hhe

### Definitions

- (v.) (of a two-legged creature) to move on foot at a rapid pace so that both feet are off the ground together for part of each stride
- (v.) (of a four-legged creature) to move at a rapid gait; gallop or canter
- (v.) to move about freely and without restraint
- (v.) to pass from one place or position to another.
