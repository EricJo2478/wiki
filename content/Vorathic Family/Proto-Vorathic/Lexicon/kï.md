- **Pronunciation (IPA):** kɨ
- **Part of Speech:** noun
- **Root:** kï

### Definitions

- (n.VI) a way of doing, being done, or happening; mode of action, occurrence, etc..
- (n.VI) a procedure, technique, or way of doing something, especially in accordance with a definite plan.
- (n.VI) a manner or mode of procedure, especially an orderly, logical, or systematic way of instruction, inquiry, investigation, experiment, presentation, etc..
- (n.VI) order or system in doing anything.
- (n.VI) manner, mode, or fashion.
- (n.VI) characteristic or habitual manner.
