- **Pronunciation (IPA):** fɑ
- **Part of Speech:** noun,postposition
- **Root:** fà

### Definitions

- (n.I) the front or under part of a vertebrate body from the breastbone to the pelvis, containing the abdominal viscera; the abdomen.
- (n.I) the stomach with its adjuncts.
- (n.I) the inside or interior of anything.
- (n.I) the front, inner, or under surface or part, as distinguished from the back.
