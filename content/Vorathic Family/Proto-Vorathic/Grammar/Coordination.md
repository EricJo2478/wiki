[[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] has 3 different coordination strategies. They are all achieved by placing a word after each [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] or phrase to mark its meaning. To achieve Coordination (and) use the word [[zoezae]], for Disjunction (or) use the word [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/Lexicon/pu|pu]] and for Contrast (but) use the word [[zaete]].
