Demonstratives in [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] are simple. At the end of a [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] phrase you can place the [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Adverbs|adverbs]] [[ngoe]] or [[koe]] to mean proximal or distal respectively.
