[[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] uses a base 13 number system cause that's fun :). Unlucky number 13. For the rest of this document I will be using a for 10, b for 11, and c for 12.
For cardinal numbers the word comes before the [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]]. For ordinals the word comes after the [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]]. The forms for both are the same, the difference is simply positional. Below is a list of the numbers 1-c, multiples of 10, numbers 11-1c, and numbers 21-2c. The pattern used for the numbers 21-2c can be used to create greater numbers.
