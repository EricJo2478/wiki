[[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] nouns are simple they do not inflect at all. Nouns are often, however, followed by a classifier word. Such [[tàghhë]] or [[hè]]. These classifiers also often end up behind a [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Verbs|verb]] just for extra clarity.

### Noun Gender

[[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] does not have inherint noun gender. Though the following 6 words are often used to classify nouns. When used to classify a noun it is appended to the end of the noun. For larger words this got simplfied into a mono-syllalic option. All the source root words then underwent semantic bleaching and have new derived forms for their meanings.
_Note: Because the power suffix simplified power was not semantically bleached._

1. "Power" ([[tàghhë]] > [[gë]])
2. "Undead" ([[nod]])
3. "Fairy" ([[loer]])
4. "Tool" ([[hè]])
5. "Corpse" ([[wà]])
6. "Rock" ([[hü]])

### Noun Function

[[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] has no inflected cases. They are marked using postpositions.

### Noun Number

There is no number distinction marked on nouns.

### Noun Possession

The [[sae|genitive postpostion]] is used to mark possession, coming after the possessor. The possessor comes before the possessee, with the possessee being the head of the genitive phrase. An example below

  <tr>

    <td>vëhh</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>human</td>
    <td><span class="sc">GEN</span></td>
    <td>apple</td>
  </tr>
</table>
