[[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] pronouns are simple. There is a 1st person ([[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/Lexicon/lo]]) and 2nd person ([[wë]]). For the 3rd person the word "thing" is used ([[wï]]), often with a [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/Grammar/Nouns|noun]] classifier. These pronouns are interpreted as singular on their own. The plural is created by placing the word [[hoe]] "many" after the pronoun as a post nominal modifier.
