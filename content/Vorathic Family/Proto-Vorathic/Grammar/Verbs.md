Verbs in [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] do a lot less heavy lifting than in [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] since they don't take any direct inflection. However there is a word order for the post verb auxiliaries. Seen below.
ROOT SUBJECT-CLASSIFIER OBJECT-CLASSIFIER VOICE POLARITY TENSE

### Transitivity

Verbs in [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] do not inflect. Though there are intransitive and transitive verbs. They are treated the same.

# Voice

If a verb is in the passive voice then a modifier auxiliary follows the verb. In the event that no passive auxiliary is present then the verb is assumed to be in the active voice. See an example below:

SUBJECT"demon" CLASSFIER"power" VERB"attack" CLASSIFIER"power" VOCE"passive" > "something attacks the demon"

### Polarity

Polarity is simply an auxiliary that follows the verb to mark negation. The positive is assumed when no negation is marked.

### Tense

Tense is handled similarly to polarity. Present tense is assumed when no tense auxiliary is present.

### Copula

[[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] uses a zero copula. For the present tense the verb is omitted entirely in the positive. For other tenses use the auxiliary verbs alone with no base verb.
