Verbs in [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] do a lot less heavy lifting than in [[Vorathic Family/Vorathic/index|Vorathic]] since they don't take any direct inflection. However there is a word order for the post verb auxiliaries. Seen below.

### Transitivity
Verbs in [[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] do not inflect. Though there are intransitive and transitive verbs. They are treated the same. 
# Voice
If a verb is in the passive voice then a [[bà|modifier auxiliary]] follows the verb. In the event that no passive auxiliary is present then the verb is assumed to be in the active voice. See an example below:

### Polarity

Polarity is simply an [[goe|auxiliary]] that follows the verb to mark negation. The positive is assumed when no negation is marked.

### Tense

Tense is handled similarly to polarity with an auxiliary for the [[vi|past]] and [[voe|future]]. Present tense is assumed when no tense auxiliary is present.

### Copula

[[Vorathic Family/Proto-Vorathic/index|Proto-Vorathic]] uses a zero copula. For the present tense the verb is omitted entirely in the positive. For other tenses use the auxiliary verbs alone with no base verb.
