| Radical Harmonic Convergence  | [αback] / #(C)V[αback]…_ | Vowels in roots harmonize with the first vowel of a root in their back feature, but not in any other way. The vowels retain their rounding when possible and height always. Please note that due to Mid Vowel Spread, there are going to be reduced options for these vowels.  <br>i > y after u  <br>y > u after u/ɑ  <br>æ > ɑ after u/ɑ  <br>ɨ > i after i/y/e/æ and u after u/ɑ  <br>u > y after i/y/e/æ  <br>ɑ > æ after i/y/e/æ |
| Non-Low Suffix Vowel Harmony  |                          | In suffixes, vowels came to harmonize with their roots.
